About Baichuan

Baichuan is a Kenyan engineering and construction company working in water conservancy, electric power and civil infrastructure. Our technical team has more than fifty years of construction experience behind it, and has worked in Kenya since 2015.

We take projects from survey and design support through construction, installation and commissioning. Our clients are government agencies, county governments, utilities, developers and private companies.

  • Our own plant. Excavators, rollers, dozers and cranes are owned and run by us, not hired in.
  • One contractor, several disciplines. Civil, hydraulic and electrical works under one contract.
  • Safety and quality systems. Documented quality, environmental and health & safety procedures on every site.

What we do

Six areas of work, often combined on a single project.

Water conservancy & hydraulics

Dams, reservoirs, embankments, river training, dikes and flood-control works.

Water supply & treatment

Treatment works, storage tanks, pumping stations, transmission mains and distribution pipelines.

Power transmission & substations

Lines and substations up to 220 kV, plus installation, testing and maintenance of electrical equipment.

Roads & municipal works

Roads, bridges, drainage, street lighting and other public infrastructure.

Building construction

Commercial, institutional and residential buildings, from structure to fit-out and services.

Landscape & environmental

Parks, irrigation, ecological restoration and environmental protection works.
