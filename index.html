About Baichuan

Infrastructure partners who build for the long term

Baichuan Water Conservancy and Electric Power Construction Limited is a Kenyan engineering and construction company. We specialise in water conservancy, electric power and civil infrastructure. Our technical heritage goes back more than five decades. Our team combines international construction standards with local knowledge of Kenya and the wider East African region.

We deliver projects from survey and design support through construction, installation and commissioning. Clients include government agencies, utilities, developers and private enterprises.
